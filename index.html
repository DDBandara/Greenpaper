NSBM students win the national robotics challenge

A team from the Faculty of Computing took first place after a two-day final, beating 24 universities.
By Computing Society  |  02 Oct 2026  |  4 min read
The winning team after the final run.

A team from the Faculty of Computing took first place at the national robotics challenge, finishing ahead of 24 other universities after a two-day final in Colombo.

The students built the robot over three months, testing it in the corridors of the engineering block after classes. Their lead programmer said the final run came down to a single sensor adjustment made minutes before the start.

The team will now represent Sri Lanka at the regional round next March. The Computing Society is looking for first-year students who want to join the build team.
